* Working notes
- Reproducibility-field vocabulary:
  - `Code availability`: `repo`, `artifact_only`, `private`, `promised`, `withdrawn`, `none`
  - `Dependency openness`: `open`, `mixed`, `proprietary`
- `Board dependency`: normalized board token used only for quick filtering
- `Board dependency` tokens are normalized by hand to keep filtering stable:
  - `board_agnostic`, `fpga_generic`, `zcu102`, `u280`, `kv260`, `zynq_7000`, `edge_fpga_unspecified`, `fpga_asic_prototype`, etc.
- `Parameter completeness`: `na`, `partial`, `complete`
- `Rerun actionability`: `yes`, `conditional`, `no`
- `Reproducibility signal`: `low`, `medium`, `high`
- `Artifact quality`: `low`, `medium`, `high`
- Prioritize papers that are both open and practical for open-source Kintex-480T workflows:
  - XtraMAC (ID 21)
  - MASE (ID 9)
  - Spatial acceleration (ID 12)
  - HLSTransform / HLS with MLIR-CIRCT (ID 11, 14) as method references
